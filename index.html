/agent-m · product

Meet Agent M. It watches every job and fixes what breaks.

Agent M is our self-healing monitor for Python and BigQuery jobs. It records every run, reads the logs when something fails, and writes the fix. Safe fixes apply on their own. Everything else goes to your team with the diagnosis already done.

  1. Capture

    Every run writes its metadata to tables: start, end, rows, retries, warnings and errors.

  2. Detect

    Failures, slow runs, missed SLAs and unusual row counts are flagged as they happen.

  3. Diagnose

    A log-analysis agent compares the failure with schema history and past runs to find the cause.

  4. Heal

    It writes the fix query, such as an ALTER TABLE for schema drift, and retries the job.

  5. Learn

    Each fix is logged with its reason, so you get an audit trail and fewer repeat failures.
